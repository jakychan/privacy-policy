Delete Your Sky Hopper Account

Sky Hopper respects your privacy and gives you the ability to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

If you no longer wish to use Sky Hopper, you may request permanent deletion of your account and your game data by following the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Please send an email to:

Email:
lechicong277@gmail.com

Email Subject:
Sky Hopper Account Deletion Request

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Your Google account email used to sign in.
  • Your player nickname (if available).
  • Your approximate last login date (optional).

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your request is processed, the following information associated with your Sky Hopper account will be permanently deleted:

  • Player profile
  • Saved game progress
  • Cloud save data
  • Leaderboard records
  • High scores
  • Achievements stored for the game
  • Authentication information associated with Sky Hopper
  • Any other game-related data stored in our Firebase services

What Data May Be Retained

Certain anonymous technical information may be retained for a limited period for legal, fraud prevention, security, or operational purposes, including:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Anonymous analytics data (Firebase Analytics)

These records do not contain information that can be used to continue using your Sky Hopper account after deletion.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 7 business days.

After the deletion process has been completed, your game data cannot be recovered.
