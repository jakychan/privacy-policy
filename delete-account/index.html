Delete Sky Hopper Account

Sky Hopper users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to lechicong277@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: Sky Hopper Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the Google account email used to sign in to Sky Hopper

Data that will be deleted

  • Player profile
  • High score and leaderboard data
  • Coins, levels, achievements, rewards and cloud-save data
  • Firebase authentication account associated with the game

Processing time

Deletion requests will normally be completed within 7 business days.
