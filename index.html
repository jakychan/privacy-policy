Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Sky Hopper ("the Game") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and your rights when using the Game.

1. Information We Collect

The Game itself does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

However, the Game uses trusted third-party services that may automatically collect certain anonymous information to improve gameplay, stability, and user experience.

The collected information may include:
  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Country or region
  • Language settings
  • App version
  • Crash reports
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Advertising identifiers (when advertisements are enabled)

2. Third-Party Services

The Game may use the following Google services:
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob (current or future versions)
These services may collect anonymous technical information according to Google's privacy policies.

3. Advertising

The Game may display advertisements using Google AdMob. When advertisements are enabled, Google may collect anonymous advertising identifiers, device information, and usage statistics to provide relevant advertisements.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve the quality and stability of the Game, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect anonymous diagnostic information, including:

  • Crash logs
  • Performance information
  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • Country
  • Gameplay events
No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience. The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove such information if necessary.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect collected information using industry-standard security practices provided by Google services.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:
  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data (where applicable)
  • Object to certain processing activities
Please contact us if you have any privacy-related questions.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
